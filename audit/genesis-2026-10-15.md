### what blocks the bostrom root

Not row 43. `state::load` fills every dimension first and would commit once
(the bulk-build path); the per-operation recommit measured on 2026-09-27
(`cybergraph/audit/import-commit-cost-2026-09-27.md`) is never entered, and
the whole load is a minute.

The block is the commitment size cap: `lens/brakedown/src/lib.rs`
`MAX_VARIABLES = 20` — one dimension commits as a multilinear polynomial of
at most 2^20 field elements, and `commit_fields` panics past it (hit on the
first run; the builder now reports instead). bostrom needs:

| dimension | fields | needs |
|---|---|---|
| particles | 66,692,923 | 2^26 |
| balances | 29,603,586 | 2^25 |
| axons_in | 27,919,938 | 2^25 |
| axons_out | 27,273,009 | 2^25 |
| neurons | 664,975 | fits |
| coins | 444 | fits |

Two engine inflations sit inside those numbers and are worth removing before
the cap is lifted: `BbgState::insert` writes a zero balance for both
endpoints of every link (3,289,287 balance entries for 93,472 real), and
every link becomes an axon-particle record in `particles` (4.76 M entries for
3.14 M content particles). chain.md §3.3 expects a hemera-digest fallback
above `BRAKEDOWN_EVAL_CAP = 2^16`; the pinned bbg has no such cap and no
`NeuronRecord.nonce` — both are bbg changes this pipeline follows, and both
move the pussy root when they land.
