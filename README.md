the software stack of [[cyber]]. every web had one — web1 ran on LAMP, web2 on React + Node + Postgres, web3 on Solidity + EVM + RPC. soft3 is the stack where knowledge itself is the asset: every computation leaves a [[cryptographic proof]], every piece of meaning carries a measurable weight.

what you can do here and nowhere else: keep your keys and your slice of the graph, with no platform between you and your knowledge. query the graph and get the answer with a proof attached — verification instead of trust in whoever runs the server. cast [[cyberlinks]] that are signed and staked, and earn [[rewards]] when the [[focus]] of others flows through them. connect two systems by adding a link, not by writing an API.

```bash
cargo install soft3
soft3 sync   # → space-pussy @ https://rpc.space-pussy.cybernode.ai
```

one binary puts the live graph under your fingers. space-pussy is a chaosnet — everything on it is test, [[rewards]] included. full guide: [[install]]
