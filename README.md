a planetary mind is being assembled right now: models trained on everything humanity ever wrote, feeds deciding what the world pays attention to. it is being built as a black box on rented servers — you cannot verify its answers, correct its drift, or own a single thought you taught it.

soft3 is the substrate for building that mind the other way. one shared graph: whatever anyone says becomes a signed, staked [[cyberlink]] between [[particles]], and whatever the machine computes returns with a [[cryptographic proof]]. one trick makes it work — data, identity, state and proof are the same mathematical object, so proving is not bolted on top. it is how the machine runs.

that flips what you can own. knowledge becomes property: nobody deletes it, it outlives platforms, and when the attention of others flows through your links, you earn. agents become the second half of your mind — they live in your [[cyb]], linking and proving while you sleep, every step verifiable instead of trusted. truth gets an economy: claims are staked, being right compounds, lying burns.

and the whole stack is a dependency. import it, and your world — a game, a city, a market, a science guild — ships with identity, memory, money and truth out of the box. [[cyberia]] is the first world built this way. yours is next.

the first step takes a minute:

```bash
cargo install soft3
soft3 sync   # → space-pussy @ https://rpc.space-pussy.cybernode.ai
```

a live slice of the graph, under your fingers. space-pussy is a chaosnet — everything on it is test, [[rewards]] included — play fearlessly. full guide: [[install]]
