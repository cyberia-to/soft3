the rule. a layer is something that ships and is used on its own. the field, the hash, the machine, the proof and the state are substrates: each enters exactly once, in the dependency list of the first layer that needs it, and stays. a layer depends only on layers below it; the release train cuts candidates from 0 upward, so a red layer 0 reddens everything and a red body never touches the node.

why trident is zero. the language depends on three crates and nothing above them: strata-nebu, cyber-hemera, cyber-nox. it never executes and never proves; nox is only its target form. that is why it could ship three times before the rest of the ladder existed.

why the node is 2 and the body is 3. the node holds consensus state: it admits signals, orders them, finalizes by φ*, settles rewards and serves the public graph. a body holds keys and talks to a node: it signs, reads, renders. true-cyber depends on the soft3 crate and on cyb-core; it is the thin command-line body, cyb the full one. neither can order a block.

why a chain is a layer. the same node binary rooted once: a genesis, a token, the sibling revisions it closes over, and the machines that agree on that root. bostrom and pussy are two chains of one node.

two things this ladder makes explicit. joy takes trident as a library, so the warrior carries the whole language, not only nox. and mudra enters at the node for signatures while vault wraps it at the body for custody: one crate, two layers, the lower one owns it.
